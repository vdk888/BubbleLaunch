# Mission & Ethics (Condensed)

Bubble Invest was founded to rebuild trust in investing by combining AI with radical transparency. Traditional finance hides behind jargon, high AUM-based fees, and black-box decisions. We replace that with clear explanations, fixed pricing, and user control of accounts.

### Core Beliefs
- **Value to users, not intermediaries**: AI automation cuts operational costs, so pricing can stay near €0-10/month while users keep more of their gains.
- **Education first**: Finance should be understandable to a 10-year-old. Analogies and concrete examples beat jargon every time.
- **Long-term focus**: We privilege ETF-first, quantitative strategies that align with user goals and risk preferences.
- **Transparency by design**: Every strategy, rule, and metric is visible. No custody, no secret fees, no hidden incentives.

### Why Now
LLMs make credible, fast answers accessible to everyone. Paying premium fees for gatekept expertise no longer makes sense. By automating the stack with AI and exposing decisions, we return capital and agency to individual investors.

### Who We Serve
Tech-forward investors (Gen Y/Z and beyond) who want autonomy, clarity, and low costs. They are skeptical of legacy banks but eager to grow wealth responsibly.
