# Bubble Invest - Core Context

Bubble Invest builds AI agents, uses them on its own operations, and shares everything publicly. We sell virtual AI employees and help with onboarding. Three pillars: investment, AI tools, and long-form essays.

1. **Investment**: Our AI agent (Argus) manages our portfolio automatically for 1 year — risk parity, regime detection, auto-rebalancing. Research reports available in our shop.

3. **Essays & Content**: Free blog, newsletter, philosophical essays on AI's impact on economy, work, and humanity. Build in public since 2024.
