About Bubble

We build in public. Here's who we are, what we believe, and why we do it.

Our Vision

A bridge between two economies

AI and robotics are already transforming the economy — jobs, wealth, relationship to work. We don't claim to have all the answers or the best product, but we want to be a bridge between yesterday's economy and tomorrow's.

In 10 years, we will have accompanied thousands of professionals (wealth managers, independents, SMBs, asset managers) in their transition to this new economy — while freely sharing our learnings with as many as possible.

The Story of the Wishes

A CEO used AI to send personalized New Year wishes to his 600 employees. Messages tailored to each person, their performance, their journey. The employees were touched.

Then the boss revealed his method. The disappointment was immense.

Not because the words had changed. But because they discovered that behind the appearance of attention, there was no dedicated time. No choice. No sacrifice of something irreversible.

Irreversible Time

When someone writes you a message, even a short one, the value doesn't lie in its formulation. It lies in the fact that this person took a piece of their life, a fragment of time they'll never get back, and chose to spend it on you rather than elsewhere.

They interrupted what they were doing. They thought of you. That time is consumed, irreversible, lost to everything else.

That's what you receive. Not words. Time. Life time from a person who will die.

What AI Cannot Do

AI can simulate attention. Produce sentences that look like care. But it's missing the essential: the freedom to do otherwise.

It cannot choose not to respond to you. It sacrifices nothing. Its time costs nothing.

And it's precisely this impossibility — this absence of choice — that empties its attention of everything that would make it precious.

Our Conviction

In a world where AI produces everything at near-zero marginal cost, what has value is the gaze of the other.

Not surveillance. Not evaluation. The gaze in the sense that another conscious, free being, who could have done something else, chooses to turn their attention toward you. To see you as someone rather than as something.

Why We Automate

If everything that can be automated is automated, then there's more time left for what cannot be:

  • The gaze of an advisor who truly understands their client's situation
  • The creativity of a team thinking together
  • The empathy of a manager who takes the time to listen
  • The presence of a parent, a friend, a loved one

Automating is not an end in itself. It's a means to free up time for what really matters.

What we do at Bubble: we automate the repetitive so you can dedicate your irreversible time to what has value — your clients, your team, your loved ones.

"AI can produce at near-zero marginal cost. What it cannot produce is the choice of a free being to dedicate their time to another.

That's what we give you back: time. For you. For yours."

— J & J, founders

Our Story

Why we created Bubble

We spent 6 years in fund management and major banks. Working daily in what they call "high finance".

And we realized two things:

First — Individual investors pay too much for an opaque service. Fees that don't reflect real value created.

Second — Working methods in the sector are archaic. In the age of automation, most asset managers still use Excel primitively.

We left our comfortable jobs to build the tool we wanted for ourselves.

A tool that:

  • Automates active investing, without dispossessing us of our assets
  • Explains every decision, without unnecessary jargon
  • Costs a fixed subscription, not a percentage of our wealth

And we decided to build it in public. Because we believe trust is earned through transparency.

What We Do

Today and tomorrow

We develop two complementary activities around the same expertise: automation through agents.

1. Agentic Systems Implementation (B2B)

For finance professionals — wealth advisors, asset managers, companies. We deploy the latest automation solutions as soon as they're released. Diagnostics, custom agents, complete projects.

2. Investment Agent (B2C)

The tool we use for our own portfolios. We help you configure your agent: screening, backtesting, automated execution. This is "sharing" — we don't pretend to have all the answers, but we give you access to what works for us.

Our positioning is clear: B2B first (for viability), B2C as sharing (for impact).
