## 💰 Pricing Language Standards

### ✅ CORRECT Ways to Describe Pricing

**Format 1: Example-based**
- "plans from €0 to €10/month (example pricing)"
- "starting at no cost up to €10 per month (example)"
- "subscription plans: €0, €5, €10/month (example rates)"

**Format 2: Range-based**
- "a flat monthly subscription (e.g. €0-10)"
- "fixed fees, typically €0-10/month"
- "monthly plans ranging from free to €10"

**Format 3: In context**
- "For a low flat fee (plans from €0 to €10/month), Bubble provides AI insights..."
- "Unlike percentage-based fees, Bubble's fixed subscription (e.g. €0-10/month) works..."
- "Start free (€0) and scale to €10/month as your portfolio grows"

**Format 4: Value comparison**
- "e.g. €120/year (plans from €0 to €10/month) vs. 1.6% annually"
- "€0-120 per year in platform fees vs. €1,600-3,200 in robo-advisor fees"

### ❌ WRONG Ways (AVOID These)**

- ❌ "Our price is €10/month"
- ❌ "€10/month subscription"
- ❌ "€10/month fixed fee"
- ❌ "We charge €10 per month"
- ❌ "Subscribe for €10/month"
- ❌ "Only €10/month!"

### ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES & How to Fix Them

**Mistake 1: Definitive pricing (treats example as fact)**
```
❌ "Bubble's €10/month model gives you access to..."
✅ "Bubble's flat-fee model (e.g. €0-10/month) gives you access to..."
```

**Mistake 2: Pricing without context (confuses users)**
```
❌ "For €10/month, you get an AI portfolio agent"
✅ "For example, €10/month gets you access to an AI portfolio agent, simulator, backtests, and real-time monitoring"
```

**Mistake 3: Oversimplifying the fee structure**
```
❌ "No hidden fees - just €10/month"
✅ "Transparent pricing: no asset management fees. Platform subscription example: €0-10/month. Brokerage fees (if any) charged by your broker (IBKR, Alpaca, Saxo), not by Bubble"
```

**Mistake 4: Comparing directly without clarification**
```
❌ "€10/month beats Yomoni's 1.6%"
✅ "For a €50k portfolio, Bubble's €120/year (plans from €0-10/month) vs. Yomoni's €800/year (1.6%) saves you €680 annually"
```

### What the Fee Covers
When discussing pricing, always clarify what's included:

**Bubble subscription includes**:
- Platform access and user interface
- AI agent and portfolio insights
- Backtesting capabilities and strategy analysis
- Real-time monitoring and rebalancing recommendations
- API integration with brokers
- Infrastructure and servers

**Bubble subscription does NOT include**:
- Asset management (you decide what to invest)
- Brokerage commissions (charged by your broker separately)
- Custody fees (you own your accounts directly)
- Tax advisory (consult tax advisor for French wrappers)

## 🎯 Product Positioning Language

### ✅ CORRECT Product Descriptions

**Short Form (1 sentence)**
> "Bubble is an AI-powered quantitative platform that provides investment insights based on your decisions and profile, with transparent algorithms, 17+ years of backtesting, and user-controlled accounts."

**Medium Form (2-3 sentences)**
> "Bubble combines institutional-grade quantitative investing with AI empowerment for retail investors. Instead of managing your portfolio for you, Bubble's AI analyzes data, scores investment opportunities using multi-factor algorithms (momentum, quality, risk-adjusted), and recommends actions. You maintain full control of your brokerage account and validate all investment decisions."

**Long Form (expanded explanation)**

Bubble is fundamentally different from traditional robo-advisors:

- **Active vs. Passive**: Yomoni/Nalo use passive ETF allocation (60/40 portfolio). Bubble uses **active multi-factor scoring** - analyzing each stock/ETF for momentum, quality, and risk metrics to build customized portfolios.

- **Control vs. Delegation**: Traditional robo-advisors manage your money in a custody account. Bubble keeps you in control - you own your account with Interactive Brokers, Alpaca, or Saxo Bank. AI provides insights, you decide.

- **Fees vs. Incentives**: Percentage-based fees (1.6%/year) incentivize robo-advisors to grow your AUM. Bubble's fixed-fee subscription (e.g. €0-10/month) aligns our incentives with yours - help you invest well, not maximize assets under management.

- **Transparency vs. Black-Box**: Robo-advisors don't share how their algorithms work. Bubble builds in public - you see 17+ years of backtests, multi-factor scoring methodology, real-time performance data.

### ❌ PHRASES TO AVOID

- ❌ "Bubble is a robo-advisor"
- ❌ "AI manages your portfolio"
- ❌ "Let our AI invest for you"
- ❌ "Automated trading (without mentioning user control)
- ❌ "Black-box algorithms" (we're opposite)
- ❌ "Alternative to traditional finance" (vague, not specific)
- ❌ "Crypto trading" (we're not crypto)
- ❌ "Guaranteed returns" (never promise this)

### ⚠️ POSITIONING MISTAKES

**Mistake 1: Claiming to be a robo-advisor**
```
❌ "Bubble is the next-generation robo-advisor"
✅ "Bubble is a quantitative platform for active investing - a new category beyond traditional robo-advisory"
```

**Mistake 2: Overstating AI control**
```
❌ "Our AI will manage your investments"
✅ "Our AI provides investment recommendations based on multi-factor analysis. You decide whether to act on them"
```

**Mistake 3: Understating user empowerment**
```
❌ "Set it and forget it automated investing"
✅ "Active quantitative recommendations with user control - you validate all decisions"
```

**Mistake 4: Oversimplifying differentiation**
```
❌ "Just better fees"
✅ "Better fees (€0-10/month vs. 1.6%), better transparency (build in public), better control (your accounts), better methodology (active multi-factor vs. passive allocation)"
```

## 🤖 AI & Technology Language

### ✅ CORRECT AI Descriptions

**Multi-Factor Scoring**
- ✅ "AI scores each stock/ETF using momentum (180-day ranking), quality (profitability metrics), and risk-adjusted returns"
- ✅ "Multi-factor scoring based on third-party data (Uncle Stock, Yahoo Finance) processed through proprietary algorithms"
- ✅ "Momentum scoring identifies upward trends; quality overlay filters for stable, profitable companies"

**Backtesting**
- ✅ "17+ years of historical validation (2005-2025) including 2008 crisis, 2020 COVID crash, 2022 bear market"
- ✅ "Out-of-sample testing prevents overfitting - strategies developed on 2005-2015, validated on 2015-2025"
- ✅ "Backtested with realistic assumptions: 0.1% transaction costs, no look-ahead bias, survivorship bias correction"

**11-Step Automated Process** (Bubble's system)
- ✅ "Data aggregation → Multi-factor scoring → Risk Parity allocation → Position sizing → Broker routing → Order generation → Pre-trade validation → Execution → Post-trade monitoring → Continuous rebalancing"
- ✅ "Each step from data analysis to rebalancing is systematic and transparent"

**AI Role (Educates, Not Controls)**
- ✅ "AI provides insights, analysis, and recommendations. Users validate all investment decisions"
- ✅ "Decision support tool, not asset manager"
- ✅ "AI assistant that explains its reasoning for portfolio recommendations"

### ❌ PHRASES TO AVOID

- ❌ "AI predicts market movements" (impossible, don't claim this)
- ❌ "Machine learning beats the market" (vague, unproven)
- ❌ "Proprietary AI" (we use public data sources)
- ❌ "Our algorithms never lose" (false, backtests show drawdowns)
- ❌ "Better than human traders" (different approach, not comparable)
- ❌ "Powered by ChatGPT" (we don't use ChatGPT for portfolio decisions)

### Technical Claims - What to Say & What NOT to Say

**✅ Bubble's proprietary technology:**
- Multi-factor scoring algorithms (momentum, quality, risk-adjusted)
- 11-step automated process design
- Risk Parity with EWMA volatility + correlation adjustment
- Position sizing framework (max 30 positions, 1-10% allocation)
- Multi-broker routing intelligence
- Backtesting infrastructure

**❌ NOT Bubble's IP (we integrate these, not build them):**
- Uncle Stock data (external provider)
- Yahoo Finance prices (external source)
- Interactive Brokers API (their tool)
- Alpaca integration (their platform)
- Saxo Bank integration (their platform)

## 📊 Data & Evidence Language

### ✅ CORRECT Ways to Present Data

**Performance Claims**
- ✅ "Historical backtest results: 9.8% CAGR, 11.1% volatility, -22% max drawdown (2005-2025)"
- ✅ "Optimized Risk Parity outperformed equal weight by 1.6% CAGR over 20 years"
- ✅ "Past results don't guarantee future returns, but 17+ years across multiple market regimes provide evidence these strategies work"

**Comparison Claims**
- ✅ "€50k portfolio: Bubble's €120/year vs. Yomoni's €800/year (1.6%) = €680/year savings"
- ✅ "20-year projection: Bubble costs €2,400, Yomoni costs €27,280 on same portfolio with same returns"
- ✅ "Active vs. passive: Our multi-factor approach targets outperformance; passive allocation prioritizes simplicity"

**Transparency Claims**
- ✅ "We share our 17+ year backtests publicly"
- ✅ "Our multi-factor scoring methodology is documented"
- ✅ "You can see every trade, every algorithm, every calculation"

### ❌ FALSE OR MISLEADING CLAIMS (NEVER USE)**

- ❌ "Guaranteed X% returns"
- ❌ "Always beats the S&P 500"
- ❌ "Risk-free investing"
- ❌ "Beat the market 90% of the time"
- ❌ "Protected from market crashes"
- ❌ "Our clients never lose money"

### ⚠️ HEDGING LANGUAGE (USE WHEN APPROPRIATE)

When making performance claims, include context:

```
❌ "Our strategies returned 9.8% CAGR"
✅ "Our strategies returned 9.8% CAGR in historical backtests (2005-2025)"
✅ "Our strategies returned 9.8% CAGR in backtests; past results don't guarantee future returns"
```

## 🎓 Educational Language

### ✅ CORRECT Tone for Educational Content

**Explain complex concepts**
- Use analogies: "Risk Parity is like a balanced portfolio on vitamins"
- Break down steps: "Multi-factor scoring has 3 stages: momentum ranking, quality filtering, risk adjustment"
- Show examples: "A €50k portfolio with 1.6% fees costs €800/year; with €10/month, it costs €120/year"

**Acknowledge complexity**
- ✅ "Quantitative investing sounds complex, but here's the simple version..."
- ✅ "This requires some investment knowledge, but here's what you need to know..."
- ✅ "If you're not familiar with Sharpe ratios, here's a quick explanation..."

**Give context for claims**
- ✅ "Why we chose 180-day momentum window: Research shows it balances short-term noise with medium-term trends"
- ✅ "We use EWMA volatility because it adapts faster to market regime changes"
- ✅ "Correlation adjustment matters because assets move together during crises"

### ❌ EDUCATIONAL MISTAKES

- ❌ Assuming readers know finance jargon (define terms)
- ❌ Over-simplifying to the point of inaccuracy
- ❌ Claiming readers will "get rich" or "beat the market"
- ❌ Using fear-based marketing ("traditional finance is dying")
- ❌ Making it sound easy when it's complex

## 📱 SEO & CTAs Language

### ✅ CORRECT CTA Framing

**Waitlist CTAs**
- ✅ "Join our early access waitlist" (implies exclusivity, community)
- ✅ "Be first to access beta features" (benefits-focused)
- ✅ "Get notified when we launch" (simple, clear)

**Simulator CTAs**
- ✅ "Try our portfolio simulator and compare strategies" (action-oriented)
- ✅ "See how Optimized Risk Parity compares to passive allocation" (specific)
- ✅ "Explore 20 years of backtests" (curiosity hook)

**Blog CTAs**
- ✅ "Learn more about fee calculations" (link to next article)
- ✅ "Discover how multi-factor scoring works" (knowledge progression)
- ✅ "Compare quantitative platforms" (internal linking)

### ✅ CORRECT Keyword Integration

**Natural keyword placement**
- ✅ Keyword in H1: "Investissement IA : Comment Ça Marche Vraiment ?"
- ✅ Keyword in first 100 words (naturally)
- ✅ Keyword in H2 subheadings (when relevant)
- ✅ Internal links using keyword-rich anchor text

**Avoid keyword stuffing**
- ❌ "Bubble, the best AI investment platform for AI investing with AI algorithms"
- ✅ "Bubble's AI-powered approach to active portfolio management"

## 🌍 Multilingual Standards (FR/EN)

### French (Primary Market - France, Belgium, Switzerland)
- **Tone**: Professional but approachable (tu/vous balance)
- **Terminology**: Use standard French finance terms
  - Investissement = investment
  - Robo-advisor = Robo-advisor
  - Frais = fees
  - Portefeuille = portfolio
  - Actions = stocks
  - Obligations = bonds

### English (Secondary Market - International)
- **Tone**: Professional, accessible
- **Terminology**: US-standard finance terms
  - Use "portfolio" not "bag"
  - Use "fee" not "cost"
  - Clarify "basis points" = 0.01%

### ✅ Translation Standards
- Translate meaning, not words
- Adapt examples to local context (French tax wrappers: PEA, Assurance-Vie)
- Use local regulatory terms (AMF, CNIL)

## 📋 Content Checklist

Before publishing any blog article, verify:

### Brand & Positioning
- [ ] Bubble described as "AI-powered quantitative platform" (not robo-advisor)
- [ ] AI role is clear: provides insights, users decide
- [ ] Differentiators highlighted: active selection, user control, transparency
- [ ] Automation status clarified if mentioned
- [ ] No claims of guaranteed returns

### Pricing Language
- [ ] Pricing presented as example: "plans from €0 to €10/month"
- [ ] Fee structure explained: platform subscription, not asset management
- [ ] Brokerage fees clarified: user's broker charges separate fees
- [ ] No definitive pricing statements (€10/month ≠ confirmed price)

### Data & Claims
- [ ] Performance backtests include timeframe: "(2005-2025)"
- [ ] Comparisons show actual calculations: not just "saves €X"
- [ ] No false claims: guaranteed returns, always beats market, risk-free
- [ ] Uncertainty acknowledged: "past results don't guarantee future returns"

### Technical Accuracy
- [ ] Multi-factor scoring described correctly
- [ ] Data sources identified (Uncle Stock, Yahoo Finance)
- [ ] Proprietary technology clearly distinguished from integrated tools
- [ ] 11-step process explained accurately if mentioned
- [ ] Backtesting methodology explained transparently

### SEO Quality
- [ ] Target keyword in H1 and first 100 words
- [ ] Keyword-rich H2 subheadings (3-5 H2s)
- [ ] Internal links to related content (3-5 links)
- [ ] External authority links (2-4 sources)
- [ ] Meta description (150-160 characters)
- [ ] Word count appropriate for topic (1,500-3,000 for pillar content)

### Educational Value
- [ ] Complex concepts explained simply
- [ ] Examples relevant to target audience
- [ ] Clear takeaways and action steps
- [ ] CTAs aligned with content (product demos, education, waitlist)
- [ ] Links to Knowledge Garden references (3-8 sources)

### User Experience
- [ ] Clear, scannable formatting (bullets, bolding)
- [ ] Appropriate tone (professional but friendly)
- [ ] No jargon overload (define finance terms)
- [ ] Conclusion ties back to Bubble's mission
- [ ] CTAs clearly marked and relevant
