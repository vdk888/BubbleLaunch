# Marketing Refactoring Plan - Bubble Invest
**Version:** 2.0 (Revised)  
**Date:** February 2026  
**Status:** Ready for Implementation

---

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the complete marketing refactoring for Bubble Invest, transitioning from a beginner-friendly educational positioning to a **professional platform for intermediate investors** (CSP+ tech/finance) seeking automated portfolio management.

**Core Principle:** Show, don't tell. Let users infer our differentiation through transparency and authenticity, not buzzwords.

**Bilingual Requirement:** All changes must be implemented simultaneously on **French (FR)** and **English (EN)** pages. Every modification must have its translated version.

### Key Changes
| Element | Before | After |
|---------|--------|-------|
| **Primary Tagline** | "L'investissement repensé" | **"L'agent qui vous élève, pas qui vous gère"** |
| **Secondary Tagline** | — | **"L'investissement actif, sans la charge"** |
| **Target Audience** | Beginner investors | **CSP+ tech/finance professionals** |
| **Tone** | Educational, approachable | **Professional, authentic, transparent** |
| **B2B/B2C Balance** | B2C-focused | **B2B Consulting prioritized** |
| **Hero Feature** | Portfolio Simulator | **Agent + Automation + Backtesting** |
| **Positioning** | Generic robo-advisor | **Selfware investment automation** |
| **Educational Content** | Prominent (Playground) | **Integrated, less prominent** |

### 1.2 Taglines

**Primary (Brand-level):**

**Secondary (Product-level):**

- **About Page:** "On construit en public. Vous investissez en confiance."

> *"Nous développons l'outil d'investissement que nous utilisons pour nous-mêmes. Pas de marketing gonflé — juste un produit qu'on utilise vraiment. Point."*

### 1.4 What We Don't Say (Implied Through Actions)

| Buzzword | Why We Avoid It | How We Show It Instead |
|----------|-----------------|----------------------|
| "Intelligence Artificielle" | Overused, meaningless | Describe conversation, memory, automation |
| "No BS" | Crude, unprofessional | Radical transparency, honest limitations |
| "Révolutionnaire" | Hyperbolic | "On a créé l'outil qu'on voulait pour nous" |
| "Disruption" | Startup cliché | Build in public, share real progress |
| "Innovant" | Vague | Early adoption of tools, specific capabilities |

### 1.5 Key Messages

**Pain Point Message:**
> *"Vous connaissez déjà les ETFs, vous suivez l'actu financière, mais vous êtes coincé : 100% MSCI World c'est trop simple, gérer activement c'est trop chronophage. On a créé la solution qu'on voulait pour nous et on vous la partage."*

**Solution Message:**
> *"Automatisation complète de l'investissement actif : conversation en langage naturel, backtests historiques, exécution automatisée sur votre compte broker."*

**Transformation Message:**
> *"Investisseur actif sans y passer du temps. Un portefeuille personnalisé qui vous ressemble, géré automatiquement."*

### 1.6 Unique Value Proposition (UVP)

**Final Formula:**
> *"Nous aidons les investisseurs actifs CSP+ tech/finance, coincés entre le 100% MSCI World passif (trop simple) et le stock picking actif (trop chronophage), grâce à notre plateforme d'automatisation avec exécution automatisée, pour qu'ils puissent avoir un portefeuille personnalisé qui leur ressemble, sans y passer 10h/semaine."*

## Part 2: Target Audience Profiles

### 2.1 Primary: B2C Investor Profile

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Demographics** | CSP+ (income level), any age |
| **Professions** | Developers, analysts, consultants, entrepreneurs, executives |
| **Knowledge Level** | Know ETFs, follow financial news, understand diversification/risk-return |
| **Current Frustration** | Stuck between 100% MSCI World (too simple) and stock picking (too time-consuming) |
| **Goal** | Personalized investments (sectors, themes, geographies) without hours of work |
| **Behavior** | Want to be active investors but don't have time for daily order placement and rebalancing |

### 2.2 Primary: B2B Client Profile

| Attribute | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Target** | Asset management companies, CGPs (wealth advisors), independent professionals |
| **Pain Point** | Outdated tools, slow adoption, archaic workflows |
| **Value Proposition** | Early adoption of latest automation — deployed in weeks, not months |
| **Edge** | Among the first in France to master and deploy emerging automation solutions |

### 2.3 Audience Evolution

**Remove:** Beginner-focused content and messaging  
**Add:** Technical depth, quant terminology, broker integration details  
**Keep:** Transparency, authentic tone, pedagogical approach

## Part 3: Competitive Positioning

### 3.1 Our Differentiation (Without Naming Competitors)

| Traditional Robo-Advisors | Complex Trading Platforms | **Bubble** |
|---------------------------|---------------------------|------------|
| Black box strategies | Steep learning curve | **Transparent** — see the code, the backtests |
| Generic allocations | Day-trading focus | **Investment-focused** — long-term wealth |
| High AUM fees | High commissions | **Fixed subscription** — aligns interests |
| They hold your assets | They hold your assets | **Selfware** — your assets stay with your broker |
| Institutional coldness | Gambler atmosphere | **Human** — built by investors for investors |

### 3.2 Differentiation Pillars (Show, Don't Tell)

1. **Selfware Model** — We never hold your assets
2. **Radical Transparency** — Code visible, limitations admitted
3. **Build in Public** — Share doubts, victories, mistakes
4. **Local Expertise** — French context, bilingual, local regulation
5. **B2B Services** — Automation for professionals
6. **Fixed Subscription** — No percentage fees on assets
7. **Honest Limitations** — PEA/AV restrictions clearly stated
