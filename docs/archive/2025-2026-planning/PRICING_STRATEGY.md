# Bubble Invest Pricing Strategy

## Executive Summary

Bubble Portfolio uses a **cost-plus, feature-tiered pricing model** that charges for platform access and AI memory, not for assets under management (AUM) or performance fees. This positions Bubble as an affordable, transparent alternative to traditional wealth management services.

**Core Value Proposition:**
- **Professional investment tools, finally for everyone**
- **Pay for what you use, not what you own**
- **No percentage of assets or performance**

---

## Why Cost-Plus Pricing?

### Problem with Traditional Models
Traditional financial advisors and robo-advisors charge AUM fees (0.85%–1.6% annually), which:
- Are proportional to portfolio size, not value delivered
- Incentivize managing larger portfolios over better results
- Create barriers for small investors

### Bubble's Solution
Bubble charges for **platform infrastructure costs + AI access**, not for your assets or returns. This means:
- €0–10/month covers all servers, compute, data, and AI capability
- Your costs don't change if your portfolio grows from €10k to €1M
- Bubble's incentive aligns with YOUR success, not the size of your portfolio

- 50 AI agent questions/month

**Ideal for:** Beginners exploring Bubble's capabilities, understanding investment concepts, trying different strategies risk-free.

## FAQ: Common Pricing Questions

### Q: What if I don't trade every month?
**A:** Your subscription is monthly and can be paused or cancelled anytime. You only pay when you use Bubble.

### Q: Does my cost increase if my portfolio grows?
**A:** No. Your monthly fee stays the same whether your portfolio is €10k or €1M. This is the core advantage of our model.

### Q: Can I switch plans mid-month?
**A:** Yes. Upgrade or downgrade anytime. Changes apply to the next billing cycle.

### Q: Is there a lock-in period?
**A:** No. Month-to-month flexibility. Cancel anytime.

### Q: What happens if I exceed my AI question limit?
**A:** You'll be notified before hitting the limit. You can upgrade to a higher tier for more questions.

### Q: How do webhooks work?
**A:** Webhooks send trade signals to your external tools (Zapier, custom APIs, etc.) in real-time. Available in Plus tier+.
