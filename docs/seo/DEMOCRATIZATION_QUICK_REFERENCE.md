## 🗣️ Key Phrases to Use

### ✅ USE THESE:
- "Same analytical intelligence as professional managers"
- "You become the portfolio manager, AI assists"
- "Institutional-grade portfolio management for everyone"
- "Professional powers, 1/100th the cost"
- "You validate all decisions (you stay in control)"
- "Transparent methodology (see the reasoning)"
- "No minimums (starts with any amount)"

### ❌ AVOID THESE:
- "AI manages your portfolio" (no, you do)
- "Robo-advisor but better" (different category)
- "Automated trading" (without context - people think AI decides)
- "Just cheaper than competitors" (misses democratization)
- "Beats the market" (false claim)
- "Set and forget" (no, you validate decisions)
