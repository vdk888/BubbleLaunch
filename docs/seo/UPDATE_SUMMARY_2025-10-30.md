## 🎯 What Changed

### 1. **Bubble's Mission - Corrected Positioning**

**BEFORE** (Incorrect):
> "AI-powered portfolio management platform that manages your investments"

**AFTER** (Correct):
> "AI-powered quantitative platform that provides AI-powered insights on how to invest according to user's own decisions and profiles - AI empowerment to replace traditional financial actors"

**Key Distinction**:
- Bubble provides **insights**, not asset management
- AI **empowers** users, doesn't control decisions
- Users make all investment decisions based on their profiles

---

### 2. **Pricing Structure - Changed from Fixed to Example**

**BEFORE** (Incorrect):
> "€10/month fixed fee"
> "€10/mois"
> "€120/year"

**AFTER** (Correct):
> "e.g. €0-10/month (example pricing)"
> "par exemple, €0-10/mois"
> "e.g. €0-120/year (example pricing)"

**Rationale**: Pricing is not finalized - always present as examples to avoid commitment

---

### 3. **Investment Focus - User-Driven, Not Prescriptive**

**BEFORE** (Incorrect):
> "Active stock/ETF selection" (implied Bubble decides)

**AFTER** (Correct):
> "Focuses on whatever the user wants to focus on. Privileges ETF-based portfolio strategies to keep fees low, but adapts to user preferences"

**Key Distinction**:
- User-driven focus (not platform-prescribed)
- ETF strategies **privileged** for cost efficiency, not required
- Platform adapts to user goals and profiles

---

### 4. **Automation Status - Critical Regulatory Clarification**

**BEFORE** (Incorrect/Unclear):
> "Automated trading for users"
> "Fully automated system"

**AFTER** (Correct):
> **For Founders**: ✅ Fully automated with API integration (Alpaca/IBKR/Saxo)
> **For Users**: ⚠️ Awaiting full accreditation; will enable automated trading once regulatory approval obtained

**Key Distinction**:
- Technology is **proven** (works for founders' portfolios)
- **Regulatory compliance** prevents user automation currently
- Once accredited: will help users create accounts + enable automated orders via API
