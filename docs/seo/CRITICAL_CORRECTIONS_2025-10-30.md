## 🔴 CRITICAL CORRECTIONS TO APPLY EVERYWHERE

### 1. Bubble's Mission & Positioning

**CORRECT DESCRIPTION**:
> Bubble provides an **AI-powered quantitative platform** that delivers **AI-powered insights** on how to invest according to the **user's own decisions and profiles**. It's **AI empowerment to replace traditional financial actors**, not a robo-advisor that manages money for you.

**Key Points**:
- AI provides insights and recommendations
- Users make ALL investment decisions based on their profiles
- AI adapts to what users want (not prescriptive)
- Mission: Replace traditional financial actors through AI empowerment

**WRONG**: "AI manages your portfolio", "AI controls decisions", "AI executes trades automatically for users"
**RIGHT**: "AI provides insights for your decisions", "AI empowers you to invest", "AI adapts to your profile"

---

### 2. Pricing Structure

**CRITICAL**: Never state fixed pricing like "€10/month" as definitive. Always present as examples.

**CORRECT PHRASING**:
- ✅ "plans from €0 to €10/month (example pricing)"
- ✅ "for example, €0, €5 or €10 per month"
- ✅ "a flat monthly fee (e.g. €0-10)"
- ✅ "starting from as low as €0-10/month"

**WRONG PHRASING**:
- ❌ "€10/month fixed fee"
- ❌ "Our pricing is €10/month"
- ❌ "Subscribe for €10/month"
- ❌ "€10/month subscription"

**What the fee covers**:
- Platform access
- AI agent and insights
- APIs and infrastructure
- **NOT** asset management fees (users make their own decisions)

---

### 3. Investment Focus

**CORRECT DESCRIPTION**:
> Bubble focuses on **whatever the user wants to focus on**. To keep fees low, we **privilege ETF-based portfolio strategies**, but the platform adapts to user preferences.

**Key Points**:
- User-driven focus (not platform-prescribed)
- ETF strategies privileged for cost efficiency
- Flexibility to adapt to user goals and profiles

**WRONG**: "Bubble only does ETF portfolios", "Bubble forces you to use ETFs"
**RIGHT**: "Bubble privileges ETF strategies for low fees, adapts to user preferences"

---

### 4. Automation & Regulatory Status

**CURRENT STATUS (CRITICAL TO GET RIGHT)**:

**For Founders (Bubble Portfolio System)**:
- ✅ **Fully automated**: API integration with Alpaca, IBKR, Saxo for automated order execution
- ✅ **Live system**: Currently managing founders' own investments with automated orders
- ✅ **Proof of concept**: Demonstrates the technology works

**For Users (Public Product)**:
- ⚠️ **Awaiting full accreditation**: Automated trading is regulated
- 🔄 **Once accredited**: Will help users create accounts + enable automated orders via API access
- ⚠️ **Current limitation**: Cannot offer automated trading to users yet (regulatory compliance)

**CORRECT PHRASING FOR DOCUMENTATION**:
> "The Bubble portfolio system currently uses API integration with Alpaca, IBKR, and Saxo to execute automated orders for the founders' own investments. For public users, we're working toward full accreditation to enable automated trading assistance, including account setup help and automated order execution via API access."

**WRONG**:
- ❌ "Users get automated trading now"
- ❌ "Fully automated for everyone"
- ❌ "No regulatory restrictions"

**RIGHT**:
- ✅ "Automated system tested with founders' portfolios"
- ✅ "Awaiting accreditation for user automation"
- ✅ "Will enable automated trading once fully accredited"
