## ⚠️ CRITICAL: Bubble's Actual Business Model

### What Bubble Actually Is:
- **AI-powered quantitative platform**: Provides AI-powered insights on how to invest according to user's own decisions and profiles - AI empowerment to replace traditional financial actors
- **Focus on ETF-based portfolios**: Privileges ETF strategies to keep fees low, but can focus on whatever the user wants
- **User-controlled accounts**: Users maintain full control with their own brokerage accounts (IBKR, Alpaca, Saxo) - NOT a custody model
- **AI empowers, doesn't control**: AI provides insights and recommendations - users make all investment decisions based on their profiles
- **Fixed-fee subscription**: e.g. €0-10/month (example pricing) covers platform access, APIs, infrastructure - NOT asset management fees
- **Current automation status**: Bubble portfolio system (founders' own investments) uses API integration with Alpaca/IBKR/Saxo for automated orders. For users: awaiting full accreditation to enable automated trading assistance (account setup help + automated orders via API access)

### What Bubble is NOT:
- ❌ **NOT a traditional robo-advisor** (like Yomoni/Nalo) - Different category: AI empowerment platform
- ❌ **NOT a data provider**: Uncle Stock and Yahoo Finance are external third-party sources
- ❌ **NOT a broker**: Users own their brokerage accounts directly
- ❌ **NOT an asset manager**: AI provides insights - users make all decisions based on their profiles
- ❌ **NOT a custody platform**: Users maintain direct ownership
- ❌ **NOT prescriptive**: Focuses on what users want - AI adapts to user's decisions and profiles

### Bubble's Proprietary Technology (Core IP):
1. **Multi-factor scoring engine**: Momentum, quality, risk-adjusted algorithms
2. **11-step automated process**: Data aggregation → Execution → Post-trade control
3. **Risk management framework**: Max 30 positions, 1-10% sizing, progressive rebalancing
4. **Multi-broker routing intelligence**: IBKR/Alpaca/Saxo capacity management
5. **Backtesting infrastructure**: 17+ years validation (2005-2025)

### Third-Party Integrations (NOT Bubble's IP):
- **Uncle Stock**: External stock screening data provider (like Yahoo Finance for screening)
- **Yahoo Finance**: Historical price data for backtesting
- **Saxo Bank, Interactive Brokers, Alpaca**: Broker integrations
- **Future**: AI-augmented sources (Perplexity API, LLM research enhancement)

**IMPORTANT FOR ALL ARTICLES**: Never position Bubble as a traditional robo-advisor or as building proprietary data sources. Focus on proprietary **processing, scoring, and automation** of data from multiple third-party providers.
