## 🔍 Why Bubble is NOT a CIF

### CIF Definition (Article L541-1 Code monétaire et financier):

> "Le conseil en investissements financiers consiste à fournir des recommandations personnalisées à un tiers, soit à sa demande, soit à l'initiative de l'entreprise qui fournit le conseil, concernant une ou plusieurs transactions portant sur des instruments financiers."

### Why Bubble Does NOT Fit:

1. **NOT Personalized**: Insights are algorithmic and apply to all users with similar profiles, not tailored to individual financial situations

2. **NOT Recommendations**: Provides analysis tools and data, NOT "buy this specific stock" advice

3. **User Decision Authority**: Users make 100% of investment decisions - Bubble provides insights, not directives

4. **No Transaction Execution**: Bubble doesn't execute trades (except for founders' test portfolios via API)

5. **Educational Purpose**: Primary mission is financial education and empowerment, not wealth management

### Regulatory Precedents:

Similar SaaS platforms providing investment tools (TradingView, Morningstar, Seeking Alpha) are NOT regulated as CIF because they provide:
- Data and analytics (not personalized advice)
- Educational content (not investment directives)
- Decision support tools (not portfolio management)
