How Bubble Works

A simple 4-step process to invest more intelligently.

1

Screening

Define your investment criteria. Our AI-powered screening engine automatically explores thousands of opportunities to find the best matches.

2

Backtesting

Test your strategy on 20 years of historical data. See how it would have performed before investing real money.

3

Allocation

Bubble calculates optimal allocation based on your risk profile and preferences. You always remain in control.

4

Execution

Automate execution of your strategy via your preferred broker. Or execute manually for more control.
