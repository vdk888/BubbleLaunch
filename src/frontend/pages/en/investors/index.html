Your questions, answered

How does Bubble Portfolio actually work?

Bubble is a conversational investment agent. You chat with it to:

1. Define your profile (goals, time horizon, risk tolerance)
2. Explore strategies (momentum, risk parity, value...)
3. Backtest your scenarios on 17+ years of data
4. Build your personalized allocation
5. Automate execution via your broker (IBKR, Alpaca, Saxo)

Everything is transparent: you see the calculations, rules, and historical performance.

What strategies can I use?

All strategies are available in all plans. Bubble offers 9 backtested strategies:

• Risk Parity: risk balancing across assets
• Momentum: 3-12 month trends
• Value & Quality: fundamentals + quality screening
• Low Volatility: volatility minimization
• Equal Weight: equal weighting
• Custom Mix: combine multiple strategies to your preferences

Each strategy comes with explanations, historical performance, drawdowns, and Sharpe ratios. No limits by plan.

What assets can I invest in?

Currently, Bubble focuses on low-cost ETFs:

• Stocks: SPY (US), EFA (Europe/Asia), EEM (emerging)
• Bonds: IEF (US Treasuries 7-10Y)
• Commodities: GLD (gold)
• Real Estate: VNQ (US REITs)
• Cash: money market funds
• Cryptocurrencies: via Crypto.com and other crypto broker APIs
• Individual stocks: for targeted exposure

Structure: One strategy per pocket + one overall portfolio strategy.

Do I need to transfer my money to Bubble?

No, never. Bubble does not hold your assets.

You keep your accounts with your usual broker (Interactive Brokers, Alpaca, Saxo Bank). Bubble connects via API to:

• Read your positions (read-only access)
• Suggest orders (rebalancing, new trades)
• You validate each transaction before execution

You retain 100% control of your accounts.

Is Bubble suitable for beginners?

Yes. Our conversational agent is designed to educate and guide:

• Plain language explanations: no technical jargon
• Predefined or custom strategies: choose from our proven strategies or create your own mix based on your preferences
• Personalized recommendations based on your answers
• Educational backtests: understand how each strategy behaves during crises

You don't need to be an expert in quantitative finance.
