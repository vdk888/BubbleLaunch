About Bubble

We left traditional finance to build what didn't exist: AI expertise that's accessible, transparent, and empowering.

We build in public. Here's who we are, what we believe, and why we do it.

"Where the consulting industry thrives on your dependency,

Our Vision

Guiding the shift to the new economy

AI and robotics are already transforming the economy: jobs, wealth, relationship to work. We don't claim to have all the answers or the best product, but we want to be a bridge between yesterday's economy and tomorrow's.

We transfer a capability, not a product. Every professional we work with leaves more autonomous than before, and every piece of content we publish makes the ecosystem a little more legible.

The Story of the Wishes

A CEO used AI to send personalized New Year wishes to his 600 employees. Messages tailored to each person, their performance, their journey. The employees were touched.

Then the boss revealed his method. The disappointment was immense.

The words hadn't changed. What was missing was the time. Behind the appearance of attention, nobody had sacrificed anything. No choice. No fragment of irreversible life.

Irreversible Time

When someone writes you a message, even a short one, the value doesn't lie in its formulation. It lies in the fact that this person took a piece of their life, a fragment of time they'll never get back, and chose to spend it on you rather than elsewhere.

They interrupted what they were doing. They thought of you. That time is consumed, irreversible, lost to everything else.

That's what you receive. Life time. From a person who will die and chose to spend it on you.

What AI Cannot Do

AI can simulate attention. Produce sentences that look like care. But it's missing the essential: the freedom to do otherwise.

It cannot choose not to respond to you. It sacrifices nothing. Its time costs nothing.

And it's precisely this impossibility, this absence of choice, that empties its attention of everything that would make it precious.

Our Conviction

In a world where AI produces everything at near-zero marginal cost, what has value is the gaze of the other.

The gaze of a conscious, free being who could have done something else and chooses to turn their attention toward you. To see you as someone rather than as something.

Why We Automate

If everything that can be automated is automated, then there's more time left for what cannot be:

  • The gaze of an advisor who truly understands their client's situation
  • The creativity of a team thinking together
  • The empathy of a manager who takes the time to listen
  • The presence of a parent, a friend, a loved one

Automating is not an end in itself. It's a means to free up time for what really matters.

What we do at Bubble: we automate the repetitive so you can dedicate your irreversible time to what has value: your clients, your team, your loved ones.

"AI can produce at near-zero marginal cost. What it cannot produce is the choice of a free being to dedicate their time to another.

That's what we give you back: time. For you. For yours."

— J & J, founders

Our Story

The moment everything changed

2018. We're sitting in our air-conditioned offices. One managing hundreds of millions at an investment bank. The other auditing financial processes for multinationals.

We should be satisfied. Six-figure salaries. Social prestige. Clear career trajectory.

But something's off.

First realization: Individual investors are getting fleeced. 1.5% management fees on a €500k portfolio means €7,500 per year. For an opaque service. For decisions an algorithm could make in milliseconds.

Second realization: The industry is archaic. We talk about "financial innovation," but most managers still use Excel like it's 1995. Automation? It exists, but it's reserved for hedge funds managing billions.

So we made a radical choice.

We left our jobs. First, to build an investment agent, the tool we wanted for ourselves. It was our first project, our proof of concept.

But as we built, we discovered something bigger: our real value wasn't the tool. It was our ability to master AI technologies as soon as they're released, and to make them accessible to others.

Today, the investment agent is one project among many. We freely share everything we learn: AI, automation, investment philosophy, values, and our perspective on tomorrow's economy.

And here's the twist: we decided to share everything for free.

Why? Because in the AI era, withholding information is like trying to sell water to someone with a tap. Transparency creates more value than retention.

What We Do

The Selfware Model

Two activities. One conviction: expertise is proven by sharing it.

1. AI Automation Consulting (B2B)

We help professionals (wealth advisors, asset managers, SMBs, independents) transition to AI. Diagnostics, custom agent deployment, training, ongoing support. Our edge: we master every tool as soon as it's released, weeks ahead of the market.

2. Free Content & Open Sharing (B2C)

We share everything we learn. Not just our investment agent, that's one project among many. We publish on AI and its impact, investment philosophy, our values, our methods, our perspective on tomorrow's economy. Tutorials, analyses, lessons learned: everything is accessible, no paywall.

Our positioning: B2B for viability, B2C for impact. Free content proves our expertise. Expertise funds the content.

Why We Share Everything for Free

Software is dead. Expertise is alive.

AI changed the rules. Today, anyone can build a complex tool in a few days. Software is no longer a barrier. What has value is what no AI generates alone.

What Matters Now

  • Expertise : knowing what to build, how to architect it, how to secure it
  • Time : busy professionals don't have two weeks to dedicate to every new tool
  • The human gaze : understanding the business, the context, the real stakes of a client

The code? AI generates it. The difference is in the thinking, the experience, and the human guidance.

The Selfware Model

We made a radical choice:

All our content is free: our tools, our methods, our analyses, our thoughts on AI, investing, philosophy, tomorrow's economy. We don't monetize information, we share it.

Our consulting is custom: we help professionals build THEIR own solutions. Adapted to their business. With our expertise. And we train them to become autonomous.
