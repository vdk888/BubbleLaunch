Who we are

Two backgrounds, twelve years in finance,
one single conviction.

AI is going to transform every profession. We put it to work for you with rigor, transparency and concrete results.

01 / Our vision

Care & empowerment.

AI is not magic that replaces humans. It's a powerful tool that amplifies what you already know how to do. Our role is to be the integration layer between the intelligence engines (Anthropic, Google, OpenAI) and your real-world work.

We believe in AI at the service of people, not above them. AI that is transparent, that we document, that we share, that we install with you. You keep the key, the code, and the control.

02 / What we do

Three pillars, one mission.

Learn

Our Substack newsletter, our blog, our analysis pieces. We share what we learn while building every single day.

Plug

Our store of ready-to-plug agents and open-source skills. You install them in just a few minutes.

Install

For professionals, we install AI into your work in 3 weeks max. Diagnostic, installation, support. The code is 100% yours.

03 / Where the name comes from

Why "Bubble Invest"?

In the beginning, we built an investment agent for our own personal portfolio. The bubble is about investing, but above all it's the idea of a small enclosed world where you take care of what truly matters.

Today Bubble goes well beyond investing. We install AI agents across every profession. But we keep the spirit: invest in what matters, not in overload. Small gestures, big effects.

04 / Our positioning

The last mile between the engine and you.

Anthropic, Google, OpenAI build the intelligence engine. We build the missing layer between the engine and your real-world work. That's our one and only job.
