01 / Our vision

Empowerment and clarity.

AI is not magic that replaces people. It is a powerful tool that amplifies what you already know how to do.

Our role is to be the integration layer between intelligence engines (Anthropic, Google, OpenAI) and your real-world work. We do not resell SaaS subscriptions: we install, configure, and support.

We believe in AI at the service of people, not above them. Transparent, documented, shared. You keep full ownership, control, and use of your agent. Always.

04 / Where the name comes from

Why "Bubble Invest"?

We started by building an investment agent for our personal portfolio. The bubble is the investment, but above all it is the idea of a small, contained world where you take care of what truly matters.

Today Bubble goes beyond investment. We deploy AI agents across every industry. But we keep the spirit: invest in what matters, not in the noise. Small actions, big impact.
