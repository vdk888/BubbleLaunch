02 / Understand

The hard part isn't the AI. It's knowing what to ask it.

You know your job. The AI knows how to execute. What's missing between the two is a translation. That's our role. We start from your real use case, and you leave with a written action plan. No code, no jargon.

Personalized 1-hour session

We don't teach you AI, we teach you to talk to it. No need to become a developer: we show you how to phrase what you want so the AI delivers it, concretely, on your workflow. All levels, from beginner to advanced.

What we master and unblock for you:

  • LLM vs Agent: the distinction that changes everything
  • Skills & workspaces: a job description for your agents
  • Scheduled tasks (cron): automate without coding
  • Sub-agents: delegate to specialists
  • MCP & connections: plug in Google Drive, CRM, APIs
  • Security & privacy: sensitive data, local-first

You don't have to master any of it. That's exactly our job.

Need something bigger? We can chain several sessions, or build the custom tool you're missing outright. We'll talk about it during the diagnostic.
