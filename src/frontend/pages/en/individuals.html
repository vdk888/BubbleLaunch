02 / Understand

The problem isn't AI. It's knowing what to ask it.

You know your craft. AI knows how to execute. What's missing is the translation. That's our role. We start from your real use case, and you leave with a written action plan. No code, no jargon.

Personalized 1h session

We don't teach you about AI, we teach you how to talk to it. No need to become a developer: we show you how to phrase what you want so AI delivers it, concretely, on your workflow. All levels, from beginner to advanced.

What we master and unlock for you:

  • LLM vs Agent: the distinction that changes everything
  • Skills and workspaces: job descriptions for your agents
  • Scheduled tasks (cron): automate without coding
  • Sub-agents: delegate to specialists
  • MCP and connections: plug in Google Drive, CRM, APIs
  • Security and privacy: sensitive data, local-first

Need something broader? We can chain multiple sessions, or build the custom tool you're missing directly. We'll talk about it during the diagnostic.
