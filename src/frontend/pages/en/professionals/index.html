What we do

We primarily support SMEs and asset management companies in modernizing their financial processes. At Bubble Invest, there's no selling dreams, endless PowerPoint diagnostics, or magical solutions that only work with an army of consultants. We prefer pragmatic projects: automation and optimization of concrete tasks, with simple or customized tools according to each need.

Like with our Bubble investment product, our goal is also to share what we've learned and discovered: everything that's now possible with current tools (Codex, Claude Code, low code, front-end/back-end development, custom AI-powered automations, custom intelligent agents…), to enable everyone to benefit from these advances, regardless of their technical level.

In line with our finance experience and technological appetite, we use the most reliable and current intelligent tools with an agility that truly adapts to each client and context.

Why Bubble

After years in consulting firms and asset management, we've observed many pitfalls: six-figure missions that lead nowhere, consultants who disappear after presenting their report, and many promises rarely kept.

At Bubble Invest, we chose to do things differently: focus on honesty and a genuine desire to help—even if it sometimes means saying no to a project.

We prefer to showcase our experience rather than pretend to know everything. We work with you, without selling you a technological revolution. On each project, we prioritize transparency, even if it means acknowledging our limitations.

What is Bubble Portfolio White-Label?

Bubble Portfolio White-Label is our AI-powered investment platform in white-label form, designed for Wealth Managers, family offices, and wealth advisors.

Instead of building a complex investment platform yourself, you offer your clients the capabilities of automated screening, strategy backtesting, and AI-powered asset allocation — under your brand.

Use cases:
• A wealth manager offers clients an AI interface to design their own strategies
• A family office automates multi-portfolio management
• A wealth advisor provides transparent backtesting tools to their clients

👉 Learn more about Bubble Portfolio White-Label

How is Bubble Portfolio White-Label priced?

Custom quote based on:

• Number of clients/portfolios managed
• Assets under management (AUM)
• Level of customization desired
• Transaction volume

Unlike traditional % AUM models, our pricing is transparent and predictable.

Advantage for you: Offer a premium solution to your clients without the traditional development costs.

Who is Bubble Portfolio White-Label for?

Target audiences:

• Wealth Managers: Modernize your offering with AI tools, differentiate yourself
• Family Offices: Automate multi-portfolio management and reporting
• Wealth Advisors: Offer transparent backtesting and allocation platform to clients
• Asset Managers: Integrate screening and backtesting into your strategy

If you manage client portfolios and want to modernize your technology, White-Label is for you.

What's the difference between Bubble Businesses and Bubble Portfolio White-Label?

Bubble Businesses:
• Custom AI consulting for SMEs, fintech, and managers
• Automation projects: custom workflows for finance processes, AI agents
• Pricing: €3k-€30k per project
• You have an operational problem? Bubble Businesses automates the solution.

Bubble Portfolio White-Label:
• Complete investment platform for your clients
• Capabilities: screening, backtesting, allocation, reporting
• Pricing: Custom quote (based on AUM, clients, transactions)
• You manage client portfolios? White-Label modernizes your offering.

You can use both: Bubble Businesses to automate your internal processes, White-Label to modernize your client offering.

Does Bubble Portfolio White-Label support cryptocurrencies?

Yes. The white-label platform supports:

• Low-cost ETFs (primary focus)
• Cryptocurrencies (via Crypto.com and other crypto broker APIs)
• Individual stocks (for targeted exposure)

Your clients connect their own brokerage and crypto exchange accounts. The platform provides AI-powered recommendations, and clients execute through their connected accounts.

Structure: One strategy per investment pocket + one overall portfolio strategy managing allocation across all asset classes.
