Frequently asked questions

Before booking a diagnostic

What exactly is Bubble Invest?

Bubble Invest designs and installs tailor-made AI agents for professionals. Our mission: automate your repetitive tasks, structure your data and free up your time for what truly matters, your expertise and your clients.

How much does the Quick Start cost exactly?

The Quick Start starts at €2,000 excl. VAT for a tailor-made AI agent, including 3 working sessions. The first quote is co-built with you based on your real needs. For more complex architectures (multi-agent, advanced integrations), we provide a custom quote.

How is this different from a traditional consulting firm?

No endless slides, no six-figure quotes, no army of junior consultants. Bubble Invest goes further than consulting: we build and install an operational AI agent directly from your data and your existing tools. The result: a turnkey solution that absorbs your workload from day one, without making you dependent.

What exactly does the Quick Start include?

The Quick Start includes 1 tailor-made AI agent, designed specifically for your profession and your processes. It covers: a diagnostic of your needs, building the agent (automations, routines, integrations with your tools), its installation, and your training to use it autonomously, all in 3 sessions.

Who owns the code installed at the client's site?

Everything is yours. The code, the agents, the configurations, it's your property. We sell the installation, the support and the training. After that, you're autonomous. That's the whole advantage over a SaaS subscription: no dependency, no imposed recurring fees.

What happens after the 3 sessions?

Support is included in the days following delivery to fix any initial bugs. Beyond that, we remain available for quick interventions on quote. A follow-up & maintenance package is also offered for long-term support, depending on your needs.
