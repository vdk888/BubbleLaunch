04 / Frequently asked questions

Everything a pro asks us.

Approach, offer, pricing, security, code ownership — the direct answers, no detours.

What happens to my data? Is it protected?
Data protection is at the core of how we build. Your agents run either locally on your machine or on a secure private server, and we provide an anonymisation and encryption layer that strips or masks sensitive data (client names, account numbers, identifiers) before it ever reaches an AI provider like Anthropic. You stay in full control: confidential data never leaves your environment in clear text.
What exactly is Bubble Invest?
Bubble Invest designs and installs custom AI agents for professionals. Our mission: automate your repetitive tasks, structure your data and free up your time for what matters — your expertise and your clients.
How are you different from a traditional consulting firm?
No endless slide decks, no six-figure quotes, no army of junior consultants. We go further than consulting: we build and directly install an operational AI agent from your data and your tools. A turnkey solution that absorbs your workload from day one, without making you dependent.
Why you, and not a freelance AI developer?
We combine a rare dual expertise: strategic consulting (finance, business processes) and AI engineering. We understand your workflows before we even write a line of code. And above all, we use our own agents every day — what we sell you, we use ourselves. Faster, more reliable and cheaper than a freelance developer.
Why 3 sessions, no more, no less?
Three sessions is the optimal format: enough to diagnose your needs, build your agent and train you to use it independently. The goal isn't to make you dependent, it's to make you operational. And if you need more, we stay available.
How much does the Quick Start cost?
The Quick Start starts at €2,000 (excl. VAT) for a custom AI agent, 3 working sessions included. The first quote is co-built with you based on your real needs. For more complex architectures (multi-agent, advanced integrations), we provide a tailored quote.
What exactly does the Quick Start include?
A custom AI agent, designed for your profession and your processes. It includes: a diagnostic of your needs, the build of the agent (automations, routines, integrations with your tools), its installation, and hands-on support to get you using it independently — all in 3 sessions.
What happens after the 3 sessions?
Support is included in the days following delivery to fix any initial bugs. Beyond that, we remain available for quick interventions on quote, and a follow-up & maintenance package is offered for ongoing support, based on your needs.
Do you provide custom quotes beyond the Quick Start?
Absolutely. Beyond the Quick Start, we design tailored solutions: multi-agent teams, selfware (personal AI tools), dedicated applications or complex architectures. Each project gets a quote adapted to your scope.
How long until we can start?
The first session is free and with no commitment. After signing, the project starts within 1 to 2 weeks, for full delivery in 3 weeks: a turnkey agent, ready to use on your server or locally.
Do you offer a maintenance / support contract?
Yes. Once the agent is deployed, we offer maintenance contracts adapted to your needs: a monthly package on quote for regular follow-up, or one-off intervention at €500 (excl. VAT)/day, with a guaranteed response time within 24h — remotely or on site.
What if something breaks 6 months later?
We stay reachable after delivery. Our support agents are available to guide you, and our team steps in quickly if a problem arises, even months later.
Which sectors do you work with?
We support small and medium-sized structures, independents and regulated professions, with a core focus on wealth management and asset management. Why small structures? That's where AI has the most impact: fewer hierarchical layers, fewer stacked software tools, results visible immediately.
What level of security do you guarantee?
Your agents run either locally on your machine or on a secure private server. Data is processed through leading AI providers (Anthropic, OpenAI) with their standard security protocols. All conversations are archived locally, with the option of automatic backups.
Who owns the code installed at your offices?
Everything belongs to you: the code, the agents, the configurations. We sell the installation, the support and the onboarding — after that, you're autonomous. That's the whole advantage over a SaaS subscription: no dependency, no imposed recurring fees.
Which AI models do you use?
Mainly Claude (Anthropic), while staying flexible based on your preferences. Important point: the agents run on your own subscription (OpenAI, Claude or other). You keep full control over your costs and your data.
Can the agents run without an internet connection?
AI agents need an internet connection to access the language models. However, some simple automations and custom applications can run locally depending on your setup.
Cancellation and refund policy?
The first session is free and with no commitment. You can decide not to continue after this first meeting — no fees, no obligation.
How does signing work?
A simple and transparent process: quote and contract signed electronically (Yousign). The project starts as soon as the deposit is received. Payment facilities are available and defined together up front.
Do you support our teams?
Yes, and it's concrete: no slides or theoretical lectures, but hands-on sessions where you learn by doing, directly on your agent. We don't leave until your team has mastered it — it's like welcoming a new colleague, already operational on your processes.
Do you work remotely or on site?
Both, depending on your preferences and the nature of the project. Based in the Paris region, we're available all across France and able to operate entirely remotely.
