Concrete Examples

What we have deployed

Asset Manager

Watch & Reporting Intelligence

A multi-mandate manager struggled to follow the news on over 80 positions while delivering personalized reporting to each client.

Solution: Two interconnected agents — the first scans 50+ sources every morning and generates a brief per position, the second computes metrics in real time (sharpe, volatility, drawdown, ESG scoring).

Result: 3 hours saved every morning • Client reporting delivered instantly instead of 2 days • +40% coverage on risk detection

Architect

Regulatory Watch Agent

An architecture firm managed 15 to 20 projects in parallel. Regulatory watch (urban planning, safety, environment) was done manually and caused compliance delays.

Solution: An agent that scans regulatory updates daily by project and location, sends contextualized alerts, and produces a monthly synthesis per case.

Result: Zero compliance delays since deployment • Watch time divided by 5 • Nothing slips through the cracks anymore

Equity Research

Notes & Research Writing Agent

A research team received over 200 newsletters and reports per day. Manual synthesis for credit notes took 2 to 3 days per week.

Solution: An agent that reads sources automatically by theme and sector, extracts key points, and generates structured note drafts. It also alerts when a portfolio position is mentioned negatively.

Result: −70% on note writing time • Complete source coverage • The team focuses on qualitative analysis rather than information gathering
