Your questions, our answers

We don't have technical people in-house. How does it work?

That's exactly the point. Our clients are wealth advisors, freelancers, asset managers, not developers. We're here for that: for the technical side and the business understanding. We install, we configure, we show you, and we're there for follow-up as needed. You use the agent like you use Excel. No technical prerequisites on your side, we show you everything.

How long does it take?

3 sessions over approximately 3 weeks. Diagnostic, on-site installation, then follow-up and adjustments. You're operational from session 2.

How much does it cost?

Standard pack: €2,000 excl. VAT (3 sessions, 5-7h of effective work). On top: Claude Pro subscription ~€20/mo (or OpenClaw free + variable API costs). Follow-up sessions and maintenance plans: on request, custom quote tailored to your need.

What if we want to stop working with you?

That's the goal! We document everything: code, prompts, processes. From day one, you can take over. We succeed when you no longer need us.
